📖 How to Play

🍩 The Goal

Win all the cookies. Every opponent you bust is gone for good, and when you clean out the whole table you advance to a tougher one with bigger blinds, keeping your winnings.

🃏 A Hand, Step by Step

  • Everyone gets 2 hole cards (face down). Two players post forced bets called the small blind and big blind to seed the pot. These rotate around the table each hand, and double every 8 hands.
  • Pre-flop: first betting round.
  • Flop: 3 shared cards hit the middle, then betting.
  • Turn: a 4th shared card, then betting.
  • River: the 5th shared card, final betting.
  • Showdown: everyone left flips their cards. Best 5-card hand made from your 2 plus the 5 shared cards wins the pot. Or if everyone else folds, the last player standing takes it, no cards needed.

🕹️ Your Options Each Turn

  • Fold: throw your hand away and sit out this one.
  • Check: pass the action on (only when nobody has bet).
  • Call: match the current bet to stay in.
  • Raise: match it and add more. Use the slider to pick the size.
  • ALL-IN: shove everything. If you run out of chips partway, side pots make sure you can still win what you paid for.

🏆 Hand Rankings (Best to Worst)

Straight flush · Four of a kind · Full house · Flush · Straight · Three of a kind · Two pair · One pair · High card. Aces play high or low, so A-2-3-4-5 is the smallest straight.

🧠 The CPUs Actually Think

  • They simulate hundreds of possible outcomes to estimate their true win chance before acting.
  • Position matters: they play far more hands on the button and tighten up in early seats.
  • Bet size matters: small bets get attacked, big bets get respect.
  • Draws matter: flush and straight draws get bet as semi-bluffs.
  • They watch YOU: how often you enter pots, fold to bets, and raise. Fold too much and they'll steal every hand. Call too much and they'll stop bluffing and only bet when strong.

Difficulty is how sharp their math is: Easy misjudges hands and rarely bluffs, Medium plays a solid honest game, Hard calculates accurately and fully exploits your habits.
